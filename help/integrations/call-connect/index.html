Call Connect

important

Call Connect is a paid XAPP AI add-on, not a third-party integration. Add it from Integrations in Studio by selecting the Call Connect card. See How can I find your pricing?

When a customer fills in your website form and asks for help, Call Connect phones you within moments, reads you the request, and on one keypress dials the customer and puts you through. You talk to a customer who asked for help minutes ago, instead of calling back a form hours later.

The customer sees your business's own Call Connect number. If they miss the call and ring that number back, they go straight through to you.

Which leads get a call, and when​

Only high-value leads get a call; every other lead is delivered by email as it is today. A lead is high value when it matches the high value lead description in your Studio settings, for example a full roof replacement or an insurance claim.

When a lead is not called, Studio shows Skipped and the reason on the lead:

What Studio showsWhat it means
Not a high-value leadThe request didn't match your high value description
Urgency is not in the call criteriaYou limited calls to certain urgencies
Contact type is not in the call criteriaYou limited calls to certain contact types
Marked as spamThe lead was flagged as spam
No phone number on the leadThere is nothing to call; the lead goes by email
Already called about this number recentlyWe called you about this customer in the last 7 days
Daily call limit reachedYou've had 10 calls today (the default limit)
No business hours setFill in the hours on your location
No business time zone setSet the time zone on your location
The calling number is not active yetYour Call Connect number is still being set up
PausedYou paused calls in your Call Connect settings

Calls are placed only when both sides can take them:

  • Your business hours, from your Google listing, unless you set calling hours in Settings.
  • The customer's local time, 8 am to 9 pm.
  • At least 5 minutes apart by default, so two leads never ring you at once.

Because we dial the customer as soon as you press 1, the 8 am to 9 pm limit applies to calls to you as well. Even if your listing says "Open 24 hours", you only get calls between 8 am and 9 pm the customer's time.

A lead that arrives after hours is called at the next time both are open, for example a lead at 11 pm is called at 8 am the next morning.

What you hear​

You answer and hear a short spoken briefing, then the keypad options. For example:

New customer request from XAPP AI for ABC Roofing. Sarah in Tempe needs help with: hail damage to a tile roof, insurance claim open. They asked to be called now. Press 1 to connect with them now. Press 2 and we'll call you back in 15 minutes. Press 3 to get the details by email instead. Press 9 to hear this again.

  • "from XAPP AI" names where the lead came from. It can be your partner brand's name instead, or left out (see Settings).
  • The request is a one-line summary of what the customer wrote on the form.
  • No key pressed: the briefing plays once more. If there is still no answer, we try your next number.

Your keypad options​

KeyWhat happens
1"Connecting you now." We ring the customer while you hear ringing. When they pick up, you're talking.
2"No problem. We'll call you back in 15 minutes." Once per lead (see below).
3"OK, we'll send you the details by email." We don't call the customer.
9Plays the briefing again.

Pressing 2 again on the call back sends the details by email instead.

After the conversation, if you talked for 30 seconds or more, the call asks: "Did you book this job? Press 1 for yes, 2 for no, 3 for not yet." Your answer is saved on the lead.

If your call drops in the first 30 seconds, the customer is put on hold and we ring you straight back: "Sarah is still on the line. Press 1 to reconnect." If you don't answer within 45 seconds, the customer's call ends.

Several numbers: if you saved more than one number to call, for example the office and then the owner's cell, we ring each in turn for 25 seconds until someone answers.

If the customer doesn't answer​

We ring the customer for 30 seconds. Voicemail and call screening (such as iPhone's "Screen Unknown Callers") both count as not answering: we don't leave a message, and we hang up their side.

You hear one of two endings:

  • A call back is coming: "Sarah didn't answer. We'll try again later, and email you their details if we can't reach them."
  • No call back (retries are off, or this was already the retry): "Sarah didn't answer. We'll email you their details."

The retry comes 45 minutes later by default, within both your hours and the customer's, and only once. It opens differently, so you know it's the same customer:

We're calling back about Sarah, who didn't answer when we tried to connect you earlier. New customer request …

If nobody at the business answers either try, the lead goes to you by email.

The numbers on the call​

  • Calls to you come from one of the numbers below. Save them as a contact so your phone never screens or silences our calls.
  • Calls to the customer come from your Call Connect number, shown in Studio, with your caller ID name. Some carriers take a few days to start showing a new caller ID name.
  • When a customer calls your Call Connect number back, we ring your numbers in turn and show you the customer's own number, so you can call them back directly.

The Test call button rings your first number with a sample lead. It starts with "This is a test call" and never dials a customer.

FAQs​

I tested it and didn't get a call​

Open the lead in Studio. If it says Skipped, the reason is shown there; "Not a high-value lead" is the usual one for a test. Write the test lead like a real high-value job, for example "full roof replacement after hail, insurance claim filed", and include a phone number we can call. To change what counts as high value, edit your high value lead description.

It called me again with the same message​

That was the retry: the customer didn't answer the first time. The retry opens with "We're calling back about …", and you can turn retries off in Settings.

The customer's phone screened the call​

Call screening counts as not answering, so no message is left and a retry follows. Customers who have saved your Call Connect number as a contact aren't screened.

How do I test both sides myself?​

You need two phones: one to act as the business and one as the customer. A free Google Voice number works as the second phone. Pressing 1 on a Test call doesn't dial anyone, so use a real form submission to test the connection to the customer.

What does the customer hear?​

Their phone rings with your Call Connect number and your caller ID name. When they answer, you're already on the line: there is no recording or robot voice on their side.

What if I'm busy or on another call?​

A busy line counts as no answer, and we try your next number. If none of your numbers answer, the lead goes to you by email. If you can't talk right now, press 2 and we'll call you back.

What if my voicemail picks up?​

We leave a short message with the request, say we'll email you the details, and try your next number. Calls work best on a phone a person answers, such as a cell, rather than a main line with a phone menu.

We're open 24 hours. Will you call us at night?​

No. We only call the customer between 8 am and 9 pm their time, and we call you right before them, so a lead at night is called at 8 am. The lead is in Studio and your email as usual.

Can I call the customer back later myself?​

Yes. Every lead is still in Studio and your email with the customer's number, whatever happened on the call.

What happens if I call back the number that called me?​

You hear a short message. The customer's details are in Studio and your email.

Can I pause calls?​

Yes. Open the Call Connect setup in Studio, turn on Pause calls and save. While paused, leads are emailed to you as usual and Studio shows them as skipped with "Paused". Your number is kept. Turn it off again to resume.

What happens to my number if I cancel?​

We keep it for 30 days. If you add Call Connect again within 30 days, you get the same number back. After that it's released.

Does it work outside the US?​

Not yet. Call Connect calls US phone numbers only.

Are calls recorded?​

No.
