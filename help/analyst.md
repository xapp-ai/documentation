XAPP Analyst brings your XAPP lead data into the AI assistant you already use. Ask ChatGPT or Claude a question in plain English — "How many leads did we get last month?" — and XAPP Analyst answers with your real numbers from XAPP Studio.

## What you can do

- **Report on leads** — totals, trends, and breakdowns by location, source, urgency, service type, and High Value Lead.
- **Compare periods** — this month vs. last month, this quarter vs. last quarter.
- **Review individual leads** — see a lead's summary, contact details, and full conversation transcript.
- **Compare locations** — for franchise groups and multi-location businesses, see how every location is performing side by side.
- **Check your AI assistant** — see what your chat assistant knows about your business and test how it would answer a customer question.
- **Benchmark against competitors** — see how your online booking and reviews stack up against similar businesses nearby.

## Where it works

| Assistant | How to get it |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | Add the XAPP Analyst app — see [Getting Access](/help/analyst/getting-access#chatgpt) |
| Claude | Add the XAPP Analyst connector — see [Getting Access](/help/analyst/getting-access#claude) |

You sign in with your **XAPP Studio login** — no new account or password is needed.

## Read-only by design

XAPP Analyst only **reads** your data. It can't create, change, or delete leads, schedule appointments, or change your settings. It also only knows about your XAPP data — it doesn't have ad-spend, billing, or payroll information.
