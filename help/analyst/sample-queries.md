Ask XAPP Analyst questions the way you'd ask a colleague. These examples are a good place to start — change the dates, locations, and sources to fit what you want to know.

:::tip Dates follow your calendar
"Last month" means the whole previous calendar month, and "this month" means the 1st through today — both in your location's local time. XAPP Analyst always tells you the exact date range it used. For a trailing window, say so: _"the last 30 days"_.
:::

## Lead totals & trends

- How many leads did we get last month?
- How many leads came in yesterday?
- Show me our lead trend for this quarter.
- How many High Value Leads did we get this year?
- What percentage of our leads this month were High Value Leads?

## Comparing periods

- Compare this month to last month.
- How did last quarter compare to the quarter before?
- Are we getting more leads this week than last week?

## Where leads come from

- What are our top lead sources this month?
- How many leads came from Reserve with Google last month?
- Break down last quarter's leads by source.
- How many leads booked online vs. came in through chat?

## Urgency & service type

- Show me leads by urgency for the last 30 days.
- What services are customers asking about most this month?
- How many urgent leads did we get this week?

## Individual leads & follow-up

- Show me today's leads.
- List this week's High Value Leads.
- Which urgent leads came in yesterday?
- Show me the full conversation for the most recent lead.
- Find leads that mentioned "water heater" this month.

## Multiple locations

For franchise groups and businesses with more than one location:

- How are my locations doing this month?
- Which location got the most leads last quarter?
- Show me a table of leads by location and source for last month.
- Compare our Baltimore and Annapolis locations for this year.
- Focus on the Baltimore location. _(Later questions stay on that location until you say "show all locations".)_

## Checking your AI assistant

- What does my chat assistant know about my business?
- What would my assistant say if a customer asked "Do you offer financing?"
- Is our service area set up correctly?

## Competitive benchmark

- How do I compare to similar businesses near me?
- Which nearby competitors offer online booking?
- What booking software do my competitors use?

## Ready-made reports

Ask for a whole report in one go — XAPP Analyst pulls the numbers and writes it up.

| Report | Try asking |
|---|---|
| Daily briefing | _"Give me my daily briefing — what came in and what needs attention?"_ |
| Follow-up list | _"Which leads need follow-up? Prioritize by urgency and value."_ |
| Monthly summary | _"Write a summary of last month's lead activity I can share with my team."_ |
| Period comparison | _"Compare this quarter to last quarter and explain what changed."_ |
| Marketing review | _"Review my marketing performance and tell me where leads are coming from."_ |
| Location scorecard | _"Rank my locations by leads and High Value Leads for last month."_ |

:::tip Turn reports into charts and files
ChatGPT and Claude can take XAPP Analyst's numbers further. Try _"make that a bar chart"_, _"put this in a table I can paste into a spreadsheet"_, or _"turn this into an email to my team"_.
:::

## What XAPP Analyst can't do

XAPP Analyst is read-only reporting on your XAPP leads. It can't:

- Create, change, or delete leads, or schedule appointments
- Change your Studio settings or your chat assistant
- Answer questions about ad spend, billing, or payroll — that data isn't in XAPP
