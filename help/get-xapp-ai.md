[get.xapp.ai](https://get.xapp.ai) is the fastest way for a home-services business to get **XAPP AI Book Online**: a Book Online button on your Google Business Profile and a booking page built for your business. There is nothing to install and no website required.

## Who it's for

Home-services businesses with a Google Business Profile that want homeowners to be able to book them straight from Google, day or night.

## How signup works

1. **Find your business.** Start typing your business name on [get.xapp.ai](https://get.xapp.ai) and pick your Google listing from the suggestions.
2. **See your page.** We show you a preview of the booking page we've already built for your business from your listing and website. We hand review every page before launch and can fix any style or logo issues.
3. **Sign up.** Enter your name, email and phone (about 30 seconds) and agree to the offer terms.
4. **Confirm your email.** Click the link we email you. That opens your XAPP AI account, where you'll see engagements, bookings / leads and analytics in one place.

That's the only thing you have to do. Everything else is on us.

## What happens next

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| Right away | You confirm your email and your account opens. |
| Within 48 hours | We verify your Google Business Profile and submit your location to Google. Your Book Online button goes live and we email you the link. We only contact you if we run into an issue with verification. |
| From go-live | Each booking / lead is forwarded to you the moment it lands, with the job, timing, address and how to reach the homeowner. |

Optional, whenever you're ready: put your booking link anywhere else — your website, social profiles, email signature — with a separate link per channel so analytics shows which ones bring in bookings.

## Pricing: Free until it works

- Your **first 3 bookings / leads are free**. Those are the proof it works.
- After that it's **$99/month, flat**. We never charge per lead.
- A booking is a real request from a potential customer. We filter out spam, sales pitches and duplicates automatically.
- Cancel anytime.

## Signing in

get.xapp.ai accounts don't use a password. Go to [studio.xapp.ai/sign-in-link](https://studio.xapp.ai/sign-in-link), enter the email you signed up with, and click the sign-in link we email you.

## Signing up through a partner

Some brands and franchise networks send their dealers or locations to get.xapp.ai with a partner link. When you sign up that way:

- Your booking page starts from the partner's design, with your business's name, logo and details.
- Your account is set up inside the partner's XAPP AI account, and you get access to **your own business only**.
- Depending on the partner, bookings may go to the partner, who distributes them, instead of straight to you.
